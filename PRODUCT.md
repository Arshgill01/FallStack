# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reddit users encountering an interactive post in a subreddit feed on mobile or desktop. They need to understand quickly that today's tower has been physically changed by community failures, make one climb attempt, see their fall or success matter, and understand why the community would return tomorrow.

## Product Purpose

Fallstack is a daily shared climbing game for Reddit Devvit interactive posts. Everyone climbs the same generated tower. Failed jumps aggregate into visible artifacts such as corpse stacks, mercy nails, ghost platforms, and cursed bricks. Clean clears can stabilize cursed zones. The level is made out of everyone else's failures, and each day ends with a result card that turns the climb into subreddit memory.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, cursed, compact, and legible. The experience should feel like a damaged vertical diorama the subreddit is collectively changing, not a dashboard, not a generic platformer template, and not an AI-styled game mockup.

## Anti-References

Avoid progress-bar cores, vote-to-win mechanics, Reddit/karma/Snoo theming, bot summaries, fake comments, fake demo labels, generic pixel-art fantasy, neon gradients, long tutorials, desktop-first layouts, endless-mode scope creep, and roadmap features on the judging path.

## Design Principles

- Make the shared failure state readable before any explanation.
- Make the first climb attempt physical, quick, and consequential.
- Treat Reddit as load-bearing: the community owns the tower and the result becomes social material.
- Keep failure funny without making blame hostile.
- Ship one polished generated daily tower with constrained chunks before adding breadth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable mobile-first UI, sufficient contrast for core labels and controls, reduced-motion handling for animations, and no reliance on color alone for route, danger, artifact collision, or result state.
