# Fallstack visual scorecard

Score each criterion from 1 (fails) to 5 (excellent). Record evidence, not taste-only adjectives.

| Criterion | Weight | Proof required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shared-mutation comprehension in 10 seconds | 20 | Existing failure artifact, origin, and consequence read before input |
| Tower dominance and scene composition | 15 | Playfield is the visual hero at mobile iframe size |
| Tactile material identity and depth | 15 | Coherent silhouettes, edges, lighting, and depth hierarchy |
| Artifact and collision readability | 15 | Helpful, semi-solid, hazardous, and visual-only states differ by shape |
| Opening jump and first-fall feedback | 15 | Artifact affects the jump; mutation response is concrete and unobscured |
| Mobile controls and accessibility | 10 | Thumb reach, contrast, reduced motion, no color-only meaning |
| System coherence | 5 | Shell, type, controls, labels, and game art share one grammar |
| Implementation and runtime cost | 5 | Fits Phaser/Devvit constraints without fragile scene ownership |

Weighted score = sum(score / 5 × weight). A direction must score at least 75/100 and no first-five criterion may score below 3. Asset providers additionally fail on inconsistent states, unusable transparency, unclear commercial rights, or a pipeline that cannot reproduce a selected style.
